Guarantee terms

  • For new Google Ads and/or Meta Ads management clients with a local service business.
  • The ad budget agreed in your growth plan stays active for the full 90 days. You pay ad spend directly to Google or Meta.
  • A qualified lead is a unique phone call of 60+ seconds or a form submission from someone in your service area asking about your services. Spam, duplicates, job seekers and sales pitches don't count.
  • You give us access to your ad accounts, landing page and tracking, and reply to change requests within 3 business days.
  • If you don't reach 100 qualified leads within 90 days of launch, we keep managing your campaigns with no management fee until you do.
  • For high-ticket fields such as legal, the lead target may be different. We agree the number with you in writing before we start.
