FAQ

Questions Body Shop Owners Usually Ask Before Investing in PPC

How much should I budget for Google Ads?

We generally recommend at least $1,500 per month in ad spend for a single-location collision shop, paid directly to Google and separate from management. Below that there usually isn't enough click volume to collect data, train the bidding and optimize toward booked repairs instead of guesses. Management starts from $1,000/month and depends on your spend level and number of locations — we'll go through it on the call.

How quickly can Google Ads start generating leads?

Campaigns can start producing calls within the first couple of weeks. Efficient, stable performance usually takes 1–2 months while the account gathers data, the bidding learns, and we cut out wasted searches.

Do you work with insurance (DRP) work or only customer-pay jobs?

Both. We build campaigns around the jobs you want more of — insurance claims, customer-pay collision repair, bumper repair, dents, paint or fleet work — and write ads and landing pages that speak to that customer.

Will I talk to the person actually running my ads?

Yes. You get a dedicated Google Ads specialist who builds and optimizes your account and joins your strategy calls — backed by our tracking and landing page team. No salesperson in the middle.

Can you track phone calls?

Yes. Most collision leads come in by phone, so call tracking is part of every setup. We track calls by campaign and keyword, and count only real calls from your service area as leads.

What if we've already run Google Ads and it didn't work?

That's common. We'll audit your old account on the call — usually the problems are broad keywords, missing negative keywords, untracked calls or a weak landing page. We rebuild from what the data shows, not from scratch for the sake of it.

Do you help with landing pages?

Yes. A conversion-focused landing page with click-to-call and a photo-estimate form is included in the build. Sending ad traffic to a generic homepage is one of the biggest reasons body shop ads underperform.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. We work month-to-month. You own your Google Ads account, your data and your landing page.
