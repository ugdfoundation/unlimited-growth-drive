Overview

Attorney web design built around the first phone call

A law firm website has one job: help the right person decide to contact you. Someone who has just been in an accident, been charged with a DUI or started a divorce is stressed and comparing firms on their phone. They want to know quickly whether you handle their kind of case, whether you can be trusted, and how to talk to someone today.

Our law firm website design starts with those questions. Each practice area gets its own page that explains the process in plain language, attorney bios show real people and credentials, and every page has a clear way to call or request a consultation. The site is built to rank for local searches like “personal injury lawyer near me” and to convert paid traffic if you run Google Ads.

FAQ

Questions about law firm website design

How much does a law firm website cost?

It depends on the number of practice areas, attorneys and locations. Most small and mid-size firm websites are a one-time project, and we give a fixed quote after a free call.

How long does it take to build an attorney website?

Most law firm websites take 3–5 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how quickly content, photos and approvals come in.

Will the website follow bar advertising rules?

We write careful copy, avoid guarantees and add disclaimers where needed, and your firm reviews every page before launch so it meets your state or provincial rules.

Can you redesign our existing law firm website?

Yes. We keep what already ranks, redirect old URLs properly and rebuild the design and pages around consultations.
