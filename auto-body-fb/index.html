FAQ

Questions body shop owners ask

How much should I budget for Google Ads?

We recommend at least $1,500 per month in ad spend for a single-location shop, paid directly to Google and separate from management. Below that there usually aren't enough clicks to learn what books jobs. Management starts from $1,000/month — we'll go through it on the call.

How quickly will my phone start ringing?

First calls usually come in within the first couple of weeks. Steady, efficient lead flow typically takes 1–2 months while we cut wasted searches and the account learns what works.

Do you work with insurance (DRP) work or only customer-pay jobs?

Both. We build campaigns around the jobs you want more of — insurance claims, customer-pay collision, bumper repair, dents or paint — and write ads and landing pages for that customer.

What if we've already run Google Ads and it didn't work?

That's common. We'll look at your old account on the call — usually the problems are broad keywords, untracked calls or a weak landing page. We fix what the data shows.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. We work month-to-month. You own your Google Ads account, your data and your landing page.
