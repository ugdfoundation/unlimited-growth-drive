FAQ

Questions body shop owners ask

Do I need all three — website, Google and Meta?

No. Many shops start with a landing page plus Google Ads, then add Meta Ads. On the call we'll tell you which mix makes sense for your budget and market.

How quickly will my phone start ringing?

First calls usually come in within the first couple of weeks. Steady, efficient lead flow typically takes 1–2 months while we cut wasted searches and the account learns what works.

Who answers the leads?

Your shop does — calls ring straight to your phone and estimate requests go straight to you. We track every one so you can see which ads brought them in.

What do you need from me?

Access to your ad accounts, website and tracking, and quick replies to change requests (within 3 business days). We handle the rest.

Do you work with insurance (DRP) work or only customer-pay jobs?

Both. We build campaigns around the jobs you want more of — insurance claims, customer-pay collision, bumper repair, dents or paint.

We tried ads before and it didn't work.

That's common. We'll look at your old account on the call — usually the problems are broad keywords, untracked calls or a weak landing page. We fix what the data shows.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Month-to-month. You own your ad accounts, your data and your website.
